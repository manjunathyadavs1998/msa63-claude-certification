# Exam Coverage Matrix

Every Knowledge and Skills line in exam guide v1.0, section 6, with the lesson that teaches it. Generated by `scripts/audit_exam_coverage.py`.

**Method and limits.** Lines were mapped to home lessons by task statement. `Words` is the share of the line's significant words found in those lessons, which is only a screening signal, because lessons paraphrase. `scripts/audit_exam_concepts.py` then searched for 75 specific concepts, and the weak results were read by hand. This wasn't a person rereading every line against every lesson.

**Result.** All 30 task statements have a home lesson. Thin spots were patched in AI06 L7 (context degradation, scratchpads, summarize then spawn) and AI03 L2 (few-shot for hallucination and varied document structures). Gaps closed by new lessons: Agent SDK at reading level, CLI flags, Pydantic, MCP resources depth, exam technique, scenario walkthroughs and the guide's four exercises.
