## Known limits

- **The coverage audit isn't a line-by-line human reading.** It's a word screen and 75 concept searches, with weak results read by hand. It found no untaught task statement. It can't prove that every line is taught well.
- **Mocks haven't been trialed.** Their difficulty relative to the real exam is unknown, and candidates describe the real one as harder and wordier. No practice score predicts the result.
- Task statements appear unevenly across the mocks, with TS 5.2 only twice and TS 3.3 and 5.3 three times each. The 500-question bank covers these more fully.
- Multiple-response items: 4, 5 and 2 per mock. The guide doesn't publish a share, so these counts are my choice.
- Guide exercises and the CI command weren't run. No model was called in this module.
- A newer guide may be published. The third-party report concerns three other exams, and it should be rechecked on or after 2026-11-17.
- Page numbers restart per lesson in the merged PDF, because it joins finished PDFs. Use its bookmarks.
- Some pages end in white space, and a heading can sit at a page's foot above a pushed table, such as Domain 5 in the study plan.
- AI13's planned 245 bank questions and 80-question lab were replaced by the 500-question bank and the exercises.
